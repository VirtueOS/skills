# Blackout Protocol asset families

Read the relevant section only. These bundled defaults describe art intent, not current tasks or verified asset locations. Confirm the exact asset, dimensions, resource path, approved concept, and permitted changes in the consuming project. Historical chat variants and unnamed rejected models are not standing requirements.

## Oxygen

A broad, moderately muted blue area signals interactivity. Gray-white tanks have a central blue band separated by black. Keep dark gray projecting light parts, centered O2 markings, and black top valves, handles, and separators where the concept shows them. Painterly texture remains visible without stretched streaks. An art-only change preserves oxygen behavior.

## Power station

Use yellow accents and recognizable electricity markings, including side views when present in the concept. Keep a volumetric housing and the existing installation side and scene fit. Distinguish the station from its battery. Derive width from the approved source and scene rather than historical dimensions.

## Storage

Use recognizable barrels, canisters, and pipe bundles. Pipes are gray with rust, sensible UVs, and genuinely hollow visible ends. Handles and lids are near-black; selected canisters and mounts provide cold light contrast without turning the whole set flat gray.

## Furniture and laboratory

Use gray, cold violet, and selected light panels, with restrained green or blue accents following construction. Glass, cables, and variant-specific details come from the current approved concept. Determine the requested instances explicitly before changing shared materials or furniture variants.

## Doors

Narrow interior sliding doors use two leaves, with consistent construction across red, green, yellow, and blue variants. Color inserts follow the approved design. Fit the actual opening without gaps, corner intrusion, or bottom flicker. Preserve existing variant placement, scene structure, and required animation behavior on replacement.

Wide storage gates are a separate asset. Their dimensions, indicators, and animation scope come from their own task and consuming scene.

## Walls

The bundled family names are `wall_volume_panel`, `wall_volume_service`, and `wall_volume_ribbed`; confirm their current project identities before editing. Match the existing module boundaries, pivot, side joints, and upper/lower bands. Use calm symmetric forms, stepped bevels, recessed sections, visible dark joints, cold light panels, and lower dirt. Check compatibility with existing neighbors and door openings.

## Ventilation

Open and closed variants need visibly different actual openings when requested as separate models. Use a one-sided grille, dark interior, blue edging where shown, and correct tiling. Add switching logic or animation only when the current task includes it.

## Spacecraft

Follow the actual approved ship images. The bundled direction describes a long wedge nose, swept wings, paired side blocks, upper fins, cold light armor, near-black mechanics, and small blue/terracotta accents. Give wings plausible thickness and develop sides, rear, and underside. Preserve the existing ship's dock fit when replacing it. Source filenames and camera-shot names must be resolved in the project rather than inferred from old chat references.

## Workshop and floor props

Tool stands, reels, and service carts follow their approved concept. Make tools large, recognizable, contrasting, and visibly attached. Check reel handles and cable terminations against the reference. Derive dimensions and catalog/placement requirements from the current task rather than copying another prop's history.
