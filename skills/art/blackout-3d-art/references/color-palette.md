# Blackout Protocol palette references

Read this when selecting colors, then view the relevant images. These are palette references, not geometry specifications or substitutes for approved concepts and the painterly requirements in [art direction](team-art-direction.md).

- [Modular sci-fi set](palette/01-modular-scifi.jpg): gray planes, light metallic edges, graphite recesses, and local turquoise/yellow-ochre accents. Useful for neutral-to-accent proportions on modules and equipment.
- [Station corridor](palette/02-station-corridor.jpg): light gray panels, near-black frames and grilles, white lights, small blue indicators, and isolated red objects.
- [Industrial gallery](palette/03-industrial-gallery.jpg): graphite, steel, gray panels, white lights, small blue details, and restrained red/ochre/green service elements.
- [Control room](palette/04-control-room.jpg): light neutral consoles, dark equipment and grilles, cyan indicators, and white light sources.

Use neutral planes as the base and place accents by function and approved color zones. Images contain lighting, shadows, reflections, and exposure: do not bake their black shadows or white highlights literally into albedo. Warm lighting in a reference does not override the project's cold light-material direction. Exact HEX values are not specified.

Compare an existing tile and its revision side by side under the same Godot camera, light, and exposure. Judge corresponding base-material regions separately from decorations. Account for sRGB/linear conversion when calculating color changes. Preserve authored variation rather than chasing average RGB alone.
